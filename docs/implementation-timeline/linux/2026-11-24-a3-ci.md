# Phase 13 A3: arch-e2e in CI and before every release

> Date: 2026-11-24 · PR: #138 (branch `arch-a3`) · Plan and results: `docs/roadmap/phase-13.md`, "A3 results" · Decision: D-059

## What shipped

- **`scripts/arch-vm.sh`**, for throwaway runners only:
  - **`boot`:**
    - Prepares the runner: a udev rule opens `/dev/kvm`, man-db's rebuild is skipped, and apt installs QEMU and `cloud-localds`.
    - Boots Arch's latest cloud image, checked against its `.SHA256` and grown by 8 GB. cloud-init gives the user `arch` passwordless sudo and the run's key. QEMU runs with KVM, all of the runner's CPUs, 6 GB and port 2222 forwarded to SSH.
    - Runs the `desktop` part as root in the VM:
      - pacman upgrades and installs `networkmanager nss nftables chromium ttf-liberation diffutils`, with up to two retries;
      - the networkd and resolved units are disabled one at a time;
      - NetworkManager gets an explicit `eth0` connection;
      - `/etc/resolv.conf` becomes the filesystem package's regular file;
      - `systemd-time-wait-sync` is disabled;
      - journald forwards to the serial console.
    - Reboots, waits for SSH, and fails unless names resolve.
  - **`e2e <devid>`:** copies the binary and `scripts/linux-e2e.sh` into the VM and runs the e2e there.
  - **`ssh` and `copy`:** for debugging.
  - **When SSH does not come back:** the script prints the serial console's lines about sshd, the network and the clock.
- **`ci.yml`, job `arch-e2e`:**
  - builds `x86_64-unknown-linux-musl` (the `musl` rust-cache);
  - runs `arch-vm.sh boot`, with a 15-minute timeout, then `arch-vm.sh e2e`;
  - uploads `vm/serial.log` if anything fails.
- **`release.yml`, job `arch-e2e`:**
  - `needs: build-linux`;
  - downloads `devid-linux-x86_64` (and makes it executable again);
  - boots the VM and runs the e2e;
  - `publish` needs it.
- **`spike/arch/README.md`** points to the script.

## Gotchas

- **`grep -v` fails when it prints nothing,** and under `pipefail` that would fail the boot. The script filters pacman's progress with `sed -n '/…/!p'`.
- **`systemctl disable` with a unit the system lacks stops before the others,** so the script disables each unit on its own.
- **Artifacts lose the executable bit:** the release job runs `chmod +x` on the downloaded binary.
