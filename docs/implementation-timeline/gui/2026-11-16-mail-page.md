# Mail in the desktop app, stage 1 (Phase 9 M4, D-049)

> Date: 2026-11-16 · Branch: `feat/mail-app` (on top of `feat/mail-daemon`)

## What changed

- **Sidebar:** Mail between Web Server and Logs, with an orange icon and the unread count. `--page mail` opens the window on it.
- **Mail page**, as on the canvas boards "Mail · Off, turn on" and "Mail · Setup and status":
  - **Off:** what Mail does and Turn on Mail. The first time, the page shows Mailpit's download as it arrives. The daemon reports the archive's size on disk in `status.mail.download` while `mail_set` waits for it.
  - **On:**
    - the status card (Mailpit's version and port, a chip for its state, Start when it is stopped, the switch), an error line when it failed, and the inbox counts with Delete all;
    - the `.env` lines with Copy, the line for other apps, and the PHP `mail()` switch;
    - the SMTP port (checked, then applied by the daemon), Keep (100, 500 or 1,000), and the mail folder in Finder.
  - **Toolbar:** Send test email, and Open inbox, which opens Mailpit's page in the browser.
- **Quick menus:** the status card's ⋯ menu and the menu-bar icon get a Mail section: "Mail · N new" with Open inbox and Send test email; "Mail · Stopped" with Mail…; or Turn on Mail… while Mail is off.
- **Logs:** `mailpit.log` in a Mail group.
- **Commands the page may call:**
  - `open` gains the `inbox` target. It opens the inbox URL that `status` gives, and only a `http://127.0.0.1:` one.
  - `copy_mail_env` copies the `.env` lines with the daemon's port.
  - Finder may show `~/.devid/mail`.
