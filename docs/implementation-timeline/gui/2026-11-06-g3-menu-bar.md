# Phase 7 G3: menu-bar menu, login settings, automatic update checks (v0.6.0)

> Date: 2026-11-06 · Repository: `devid` · Branch: `feat/g3-menu-bar`

## What shipped

The details are in `docs/roadmap/phase-7.md`, "G3 as built".

- **Menu-bar icon** (`tray.rs`):
  - the state at a glance (running, dimmed when stopped, a "!" badge when something failed), plus a dot for updates;
  - a menu with the status line, "Updates available (n)…", Start all / Stop all / Restart, Open Devid, Settings… and Quit;
  - it polls `status` every 15 seconds and redraws only on a change.
- **D-028, automatic update checks:** the daemon checks every `update_check_minutes` (20) and keeps the result in `status`. `devid status`, the TUI, the window's Settings and the menu bar read it from there.
- **D-031, two login settings:**
  - `devid autostart on|off`: serve sites at login;
  - `devid login-item on|off`: open the app at login.
  Both are in `status`, in the TUI's Settings (`a`) and in the window's Settings (Login).
- **Shared code:** `latest_devid` and `fetch_latest` moved from the CLI to `devid_daemon::releases`, so the daemon's check and `devid self-update` share them.
- **Version:** 0.6.0.
