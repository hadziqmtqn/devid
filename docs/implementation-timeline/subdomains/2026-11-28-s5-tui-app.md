# Phase 16 S5: the TUI, the app and 0.23.0-rc.1

> Date: 2026-11-28 · Branch: `phase16-s5` · Plan and results: `docs/roadmap/phase-16.md`, "S5 results" · Decision: D-065

## What shipped

- **`server.rs`:** each site in `sites` has `subdomains` (`https://*.<host>`, with the HTTPS port when 443 does not reach Devid) and `wp_network` (`"subdir"` for a subfolder network).
- **CLI (`main.rs`):** `print_app` prints the `also:` line.
- **TUI:**
  - `app.rs`:
    - `NewField::Network` and `NETWORKS` give the wizard a Sites field for WordPress, which `request` sends as `multisite` unless it is one site;
    - `AccountRow.create_databases`;
    - `m` in the accounts view asks first, then sends `DbUserCreateDatabases`. It refuses MongoDB and the main account.
  - `ui.rs`:
    - the Sites field and its hint;
    - "makes databases" after an account's password, and `m` in the accounts view's keys;
    - the dim "Also https://*.<host>" line at the top of a site's menu.
- **App (`app.js`):**
  - `siteDetail`: the Subdomains row with its tooltip;
  - the New project sheet: `np.multisite`, the Sites select, the fact about adding a site, the ready view's Network row, and `multisite` in `new_project`;
  - `addDbAccount`: the "May make databases" checkbox;
  - `dbAccountRow`: the line and the menu item;
  - `toggleDbCreate` and `dbCreateAbout`.
- **Release:** version 0.23.0-rc.1; `release.yml`'s notes name the subdomains, `--multisite` and accounts that may make databases.
- **Tests:** the TUI's wizard (the Sites field, and the field reset when the template changes) and its accounts view (`m`).
