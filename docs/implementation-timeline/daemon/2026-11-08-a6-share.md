# Phase 8 A6: Share through a Cloudflare quick tunnel (v0.12.0)

> Date: 2026-11-08 · Branch: `feat/a6-share`

## What shipped

- **D-037, Share:** `devid share [site]` runs the cloudflared already on the Mac (`/usr/local/bin`, `/opt/homebrew/bin` or `PATH`) as `cloudflared tunnel --no-autoupdate --url http://127.0.0.1:<http_port> --http-host-header <site>.test`, and prints its random `https://….trycloudflare.com` link. `--stop` ends it.
- **How it runs:** through the command machinery of A4 (process group, log `share-<site>.log`, leftovers, daemon stop). It is not one of the site's commands: "Stop all" leaves it, and the command counts leave it out.
- **Where it shows:**
  - `sites` (`share`) and `status` (`shares`);
  - `devid status` and `devid app show`;
  - the app's Share card with Copy, and a "Shared" chip in the list;
  - "Stop sharing <site>" in the menu bar;
  - `h` in the TUI's site menu.
- **Never:** the owner's own tunnel (`com.cloudflare.cloudflared`) and its routes are not touched, and Devid does not download cloudflared.
- **Version:** 0.12.0.
