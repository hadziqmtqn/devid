# Phase 15 N4: the app's New project sheet

> Date: 2026-11-26 · Branch: `phase15-n4` · Plan and results: `docs/roadmap/phase-15.md`, "N4 results" · Decision: D-063 · Board: the Devid Redesign canvas, page "Fase 15: new project"

## What shipped

- **`crates/gui/ui/app.js`:**
  - **The entry points:** Sites' + is a menu (New project…, Link a folder…), and ⌘N opens the sheet.
  - **The sheet** (`newProject`, `npForm`, `npJob`, `npReady`):
    - the template list from `new_templates`, with the daemon's limits;
    - the name checks, including the accounts of a database that runs;
    - the folder, the PHP version, the kit or Symfony's kind, and the database;
    - the facts lines.
  - **The job:**
    - `new_project`, with `new_project_progress`, `log_read` and a first database download polled every 0.5 s;
    - Hide, and a toast when the job ends;
    - the Ready and Failed views.
- **`crates/gui/ui/app.css`:** the `np-*` styles, on the app's tokens, in both themes.
- **`crates/gui/src/api.rs`:**
  - `copy_text`, for WordPress's admin password;
  - `sites_folder`, which makes `~/Sites`;
  - `choose_folder`'s `new` purpose;
  - `Open::Site`'s `path`, checked by `site_page`;
  - `new_templates` and `new_project_progress` count as reads, so they leave the menu-bar icon alone.
  - `build.rs` and `capabilities/main.json` allow the two new commands.
- **`crates/daemon/src/server.rs`:** `new_templates` reports `node`, the version the job would use.

## Checked

- `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test` pass, with the app's crate included. `node --check` passes on `app.js`.
- **In a browser** with the real page and a fake daemon: every view on the board, the + menu, ⌘N, Hide, a failure, no Node, the arrow keys, Escape, and both themes (`phase-15.md`, "N4 results").
